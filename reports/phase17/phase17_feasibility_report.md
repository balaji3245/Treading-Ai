## Executive Summary & Research Gate Verdict

Following the negative out-of-fold economic and predictive results established in Phase 16, this Phase 17 diagnostic investigates the structural root causes of failure across the Treading-Ai research architecture.

### Research Continuation Gate Verdict:
# **STOP CURRENT APPROACH**

### Primary Audit Findings:
1. **Transaction Cost Dominance:** Fixed round-turn transaction friction ($0.40/oz base, $0.60/oz stress) consumes between **17.7% and 38.2%** of the median price move across intraday horizons ($H=12$ to $H=24$). To break even, an intraday model requires a directional win rate of **58.9% to 63.3%** on price moves that behave as symmetric Brownian noise (unconditional MFE/MAE ratio is exactly 1.000). Existing models achieve only 42%–45% win rates.
2. **Degrees-of-Freedom Collapse via Horizon Overlap:** Evaluating fixed forward horizons ($H=24$ to $H=96$) on consecutive M5 bars produces **95.8% to 99.0% serial overlap**. The apparent sample size of $N \approx 241,000$ collapses to an effective independent sample size of only **$N_{\text{eff}} \approx 2,516$** on $H=96$, and just **$704$** on $H=288$. The apparent volume of data is an illusion generated by rolling autocorrelation.
3. **Severe Feature Multicollinearity & Redundancy:** Out of 232 engineered features, **180 are mathematically redundant dimensions**. Principal Component Analysis reveals that just 29 components explain 80% of total variance, and 52 components explain 90%. Over 270 feature pairs exhibit collinearity $|r| > 0.90$.
4. **Macro Drift Masquerading as Strategy Alpha:** The apparent outperformance of Candidate 5 ($H=96$) in Fold 3 (2024) is entirely driven by passive long exposure during gold's historic +26% macro rally ($2,060 to $2,600+). In 2024, Long precision was 53.73% while Short precision collapsed to 42.33% (a +11.40% directional asymmetry). Short trades consistently lost money (-$140.96 net loss).
5. **No Actionable Edge Exists:** Available evidence demonstrates that bar-by-bar M5 directional return prediction using linear/tabular models cannot overcome spread friction in spot XAUUSD. Further parameter tuning, feature engineering, or threshold optimization within this paradigm is unjustified.

### 3.2 The Mathematics of the Intraday Cost Wall
The required break-even win rate $w_{\text{BE}}$ for a symmetric take-profit / stop-loss of size $G = \text{Median Move}$ subject to round-turn friction $C$ is given by:

$$\mathbb{E}[\text{PnL}] = w \cdot (G - C) - (1 - w) \cdot (G + C) = (2w - 1)G - C = 0 \implies w_{\text{BE}} = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 + \frac{C}{G}\right)$$

- At $H=24$ (Incumbent horizon): $G = \$2.26$, $C_{\text{base}} = \$0.40 \implies w_{\text{BE}} = 58.85\%$.
- Under stress conditions ($C_{\text{stress}} = \$0.60$): $w_{\text{BE}} = 63.28\%$.
- Actual Incumbent model win rate on out-of-fold data: **44.57%**.
- **The Gap:** The model falls **14.28 percentage points short** of break-even under base costs, and **18.71 percentage points short** under stress costs.

### 3.3 Theoretical MFE vs Achievable Trade Profit
- **The MFE Trap:** Across all horizons, the mean Maximum Favorable Excursion is approximately $1.04\times$ to $1.05\times$ the terminal price move. However, the Maximum Adverse Excursion is *identical* in magnitude (MFE / MAE ratio is 1.000).
- In unconditional market data, path excursion is completely symmetric Brownian motion.
- Capturing MFE requires exiting at the exact tick high/low of an intraday candle, which is theoretically unachievable. Fixed-horizon trading subjects positions to the full adverse path excursion while realizing only the noisy terminal price.

## 6. Research Continuation Gate & Concrete Governance Directives

### 6.1 Formal Verdict: STOP CURRENT APPROACH

#### Supporting Empirical Evidence:
1. **Friction Impossibility:** Transaction friction ($0.40/oz round turn) requires a >58.9% win rate on $H=24$, while models achieve only 42%–45% win rate.
2. **Degrees of Freedom Collapse:** 95%–99% forward bar overlap reduces effective independent sample size by 24x to 96x, invalidating standard i.i.d. assumptions.
3. **Severe Multicollinearity:** 180 out of 232 features are mathematically redundant, causing estimation instability and probability miscalibration.
4. **Macro Drift Masquerading as Skill:** Candidate 5's marginal positive P&L in Fold 3 is entirely passive gold beta during a historic $500+/oz macro rally. Short trades consistently lose money.
5. **No Edge Over No-Trade:** Across all folds and models, the No-Trade baseline ($0.00 net P&L) strictly outperforms every candidate strategy.

#### Unresolved Uncertainty:
- Whether machine learning models could possess genuine predictive value on much higher timeframes (e.g., Daily or Weekly bars) where transaction friction represents < 0.5% of typical price moves.
- Whether non-directional tasks (such as volatility forecasting, spread capture, or market making) could operate profitably in spot gold.

#### Next Falsifiable Experiment (If Research is Ever Redesigned):
If quantitative research in gold is ever resumed under an entirely new framework, it must **not** continue M5 bar-by-bar classification. The next falsifiable experiment should test:
- **Hypothesis:** Daily/Weekly macroeconomic event conditioning (e.g. US CPI releases, FOMC announcements, real yield differentials) generates non-random directional returns net of overnight swap and spread friction.
- **Protocol:** Pre-specify a strictly non-overlapping daily horizon ($H=1$ day), use low-dimensional macroeconomic features ($D \le 5$), enforce a holdout test partition prior to research, and require net profit factor $> 1.25$ after institutional execution costs.
